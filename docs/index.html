Model

How it works. For every point, each player's arrival time is his reaction time plus the straight-line distance (from where his current movement carries him) divided by running speed. A smooth comparison of the two teams' arrival times gives the probability that the attacking team gets there first. The four parameters were fitted on passes from 20 matches and validated on held-out matches. "Attack controls" weights every point by how often possession from there leads to a shot within 10 seconds, so space near goal counts far more than space near halfway.
Read with care. This model shows who would reach the ball first, not what will happen next. In the accompanying analysis, this kind of space did not predict shots any better than the ball and runner positions alone. Players marked with a dashed outline were off camera at this moment, and their positions are estimated by SkillCorner.
